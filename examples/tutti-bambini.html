Tutti Bambini Las Cruces

Children's resale and donation store

Useful finds, warm welcome, real community impact.

Families can shop for children from newborn through 14 years old, and local donors can pass along gently used items that still have plenty of life left.

Tutti Bambini also provides clothing vouchers to home visiting programs and works with local partners serving children and families across southern New Mexico.

Mission

Helping children challenged by poverty and special needs.

The shop opened in 2013 with a simple purpose: help families get seasonally appropriate, properly fitting clothing and give children a place where they are seen, welcomed, and cared for.

Tutti Bambini supports children's reading, science programs, Dress-A-Child efforts, Christmas wishes, and other local services that meet practical needs for kids.

About Sister Beth

Built by people who keep showing up for children.

Founder Sister Beth Daddio has spent years improving the lives of children and families in Las Cruces. She also helped found Jardin de los Ninos, the daycare and education program for homeless and near-homeless children.

In 2023, the Las Cruces Sun-News chose Sister Beth as its Distinguished Resident, recognizing work that often happens quietly and steadily behind the scenes.

Sister Beth Daddio, Director of Development
