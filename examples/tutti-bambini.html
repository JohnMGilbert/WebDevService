Children's resale and donation store

Useful finds, warm welcome, real community impact.

Families can shop for children from newborn through 14 years old, and local donors can pass along gently used items that still have plenty of life left.

Tutti Bambini also provides clothing vouchers to home visiting programs and works with local partners serving children and families across southern New Mexico.

What children need most

With 39% of children in Doña Ana County living in poverty, new and gently used children's items matter. Cash donations help Tutti Bambini keep the voucher program going, support partner programs, and respond to growing seasonal needs.

Mission

Helping children challenged by poverty and special needs with clothing, dignity, and choice.

For more than 10 years, Tutti Bambini has helped local children and families access seasonally appropriate, properly fitting clothing in a place that feels warm, secure, and welcoming.

Today, 25 nonprofit partners can refer families to the voucher program, and the shop is now averaging more than $3,000 every month in free clothing for children across the community.

About Sister Beth

Built by people who keep showing up for children.

Founder Sister Beth Daddio has spent years improving the lives of children and families in Las Cruces. Her loving heart and dedication have helped thousands of children in the greater Las Cruces area, and she also helped found Jardin de los Ninos for homeless and near-homeless children.

The need is real: 1 in 6 children in the United States has very limited access to clothing, and 85% of Tutti Bambini's partner agencies say they would not be able to provide no-cost clothing resources without support like this.

Sister Beth Daddio, Director of Development
